UKALAA(COMIC WEBSITE)
Built by: Fidel Katee

PROJECT DESCRIPTION
Ukalaa Universe is an interactive web-based comic reader set in an Afrofuturist world where advanced artificial intelligence has rewritten the rules of wealth, warfare, and human survival.
The story follows Jengo, a seventeen-year-old living in the shadow of soaring tech-towers in the Ukalaa Market District. When a routine day at the market turns into a violent confrontation, Jengo catches a glimpse of a reality hidden behind the neon glow — a digital infection that threatens to tear the nation apart. Once you see the glitch, there's no turning back.
The project is a multi-page website featuring:

A full hero landing page introducing the Ukalaa Universe
A chapters overview section with story summaries
A fully interactive comic reader for Chapter 1 with authentic comic panel layouts, speech bubbles, sound effects, and voice-over captions
